If you want to buy or sell shares on the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE), the first thing
you need is a **Demat account** paired with a **CDS (Central Depository System) account**.
Here's how the process works.

## What is a Demat account?

A Demat account holds your shares in electronic form, replacing old physical share
certificates. Without one, you legally cannot trade on NEPSE.

## Step 1: Choose a broker

Pick a licensed NEPSE broker. Compare their app/platform quality, customer support,
and any account fees before choosing.

## Step 2: Required documents

- Citizenship certificate (copy)
- Passport-size photos
- PAN card (or apply for one alongside)
- Bank account details

## Step 3: Fill the Demat + CDS application

Your broker will guide you through opening both accounts together — they're usually
bundled into one application process.

## Step 4: Fund your account

Link your bank account for deposits/withdrawals through the broker's platform or
Nepal's Meroshare system.

## Step 5: Start trading

Once approved (usually a few business days), you can place buy/sell orders through
Meroshare or your broker's trading app.
